How It Works — 3 Simple Steps

We've made our process straightforward, comfortable, and completely confidential from your first contact to full recovery.

1

Medical Assessment & Consultation

Visit our clinic for a confidential medical examination, pregnancy confirmation, and ultrasound scan. Our doctors will determine your gestational age and recommend the safest procedure for your situation.

2

Safe Abortion Procedure

Your procedure — whether medical or surgical — is carried out in our fully equipped, sterile facility with licensed doctors, proper anaesthesia, and strict safety protocols. Most procedures are completed in a single visit.

3

Post-Procedure Care & Follow-Up

After your procedure, you'll receive detailed recovery instructions, a scheduled follow-up appointment, and access to our 24/7 support line. We stay with you until you've fully recovered.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our abortion procedures, safety, confidentiality, and aftercare.

We offer three procedures: medical abortion using pills (up to 9 weeks), surgical abortion (10–20 weeks), and specialised late-term abortion for complex cases (20–25 weeks). All are performed by licensed doctors in a safe, sterile environment with full anaesthesia and monitoring.
Medical abortion pills are safe and effective up to 9 weeks of pregnancy. If you are further along, our doctors will recommend a surgical procedure to ensure your safety and the success of the procedure.
We use appropriate anaesthesia and pain management throughout every surgical procedure. Most patients experience mild to moderate cramping, similar to period pain. The procedure is completed in a single visit and you can return home the same day. Our team will monitor you during recovery before discharge.
Your privacy is our highest priority. All patient records, consultations, and procedures are strictly confidential. No information is shared with third parties without your explicit written consent. You may use a preferred name during your visit if you prefer additional privacy.
Most women recover within 1–2 days after a medical abortion and 2–5 days after a surgical procedure. We provide detailed aftercare instructions before you leave and schedule a follow-up appointment to confirm your complete recovery. Our 24/7 line is available throughout your recovery period.
During your initial consultation, our doctor will assess your gestational age via ultrasound, review your medical history, and discuss your preferences. Based on all of this, they will recommend the safest and most effective option for your specific situation.
Seek immediate care if you experience heavy bleeding (soaking more than two pads per hour for two or more hours), severe abdominal pain not relieved by painkillers, fever above 38°C, or foul-smelling discharge. Call our 24/7 emergency line on +256 759 983 865 immediately if any of these occur.
We welcome both walk-ins and advance bookings. However, booking ahead guarantees a reduced waiting time and a dedicated consultation slot with your doctor. Call or WhatsApp +256 759 983 865 to reserve your appointment today.
