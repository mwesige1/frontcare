About Our Medical Team

The medical team at Frontlane Medical Center in Kampala, Uganda, is led by qualified gynecologists and reproductive health specialists with years of combined clinical experience in women's healthcare. Our doctors are licensed medical practitioners registered to practice in Uganda.

Our team leads Frontlane's safe abortion and reproductive health services, providing compassionate, confidential care for women across Kampala and surrounding districts. Our clinical focus includes medical and surgical abortion procedures, post-abortion care, family planning, and comprehensive women's health screening.

We are committed to a non-judgmental, patient-centered approach — ensuring every woman receives accurate medical information, complete privacy, and the highest standard of care. Our work is grounded in the belief that access to safe reproductive healthcare is a fundamental part of women's wellbeing.

Clinical Specializations

Medical Abortion Care

Supervision of medication-based pregnancy termination up to 9 weeks, with full patient safety protocols and follow-up care.

Surgical Abortion

Safe surgical procedures from 10 to 20 weeks, performed in a sterile environment with advanced pain management.

Late-Term Care

Specialized medical care for complex cases up to 25 weeks, with extended monitoring and psychological support.

Reproductive Counselling

Confidential pre- and post-procedure counselling, emotional support, and informed decision-making guidance.

Health Screening

Ultrasound, blood work, STD screening, and comprehensive pre- and post-procedure health assessments.

Family Planning

Full range of contraception and family planning options, including counselling after abortion care.
