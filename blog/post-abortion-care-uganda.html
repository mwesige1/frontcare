Post-Abortion Care: What to Expect & How to Heal

📅 Updated: September 10, 2026 ⏱ 7 min read
👨‍⚕️ Reviewed by Our Medical Team

Recovering well after an abortion depends on knowing what's normal — and knowing when something is wrong. This guide explains exactly what to expect during recovery, how to care for yourself at home, and how to recognise warning signs that need medical attention.

It's written for women in Uganda recovering from medical or surgical abortion at Frontlane Medical Center — but the guidance applies broadly to any safe, medically supervised procedure.

Medical Disclaimer: This article provides general recovery information. Always follow the specific instructions given by your doctor and attend your scheduled follow-up visit. If you experience any warning signs listed below, seek medical care immediately.

What to Expect After a Medical Abortion (Pills)

After a medical abortion, most women describe the recovery as similar to a normal period. The body is clearing the uterus, and the process typically feels like a heavier-than-usual menstrual cycle.

Bleeding

  • Duration: Bleeding usually lasts 3–5 days, similar to a normal period
  • Flow: Heavier than usual in the first 1–2 days, with some clots
  • Manage with: Sanitary pads — regular use, changed as normal
  • After the main bleeding: Light spotting may continue for a few days

Cramping

  • Mild to moderate cramps, similar to period pain
  • Usually strongest in the first few hours after the second medication
  • Eases over the first day and gradually fades

Daily activities

  • You can continue with your normal routine — work, school, household activities
  • Use sanitary pads as you would during a period
  • Rest when you feel you need it, but you don't need to stay in bed

What to Expect After a Surgical Abortion

Surgical abortion recovery is generally faster, because the procedure is completed in a single visit.

Bleeding

  • Duration: Typically light spotting only
  • Flow: Much lighter than a period
  • Manage with: Sanitary pads — regular use

Recovery time

  • Most women feel back to normal within about 2 days
  • Mild cramping may continue for a day or two
  • Return to work and daily activities: usually within 2 days

Medication We Provide

After your procedure, we give you medication to support your recovery. These include:

  • Pain management — to keep you comfortable during recovery
  • Bleeding control — to help manage and reduce bleeding
  • Infection prevention — to protect against post-procedure infection
  • Cleansing support — to help your body clear the uterus safely

Our doctors will explain exactly what you're taking and how to use each medication. If you experience any unexpected reaction, contact us immediately on +256 759 983 865.

Follow-Up Care

We schedule a follow-up visit at 14 days (2 weeks) after your procedure.

At this visit, we:

  • Confirm the abortion is complete
  • Check for any complications
  • Review your recovery and answer any concerns
  • Discuss family planning and contraception options

Even if you feel completely well, please attend this visit. In a small number of cases, an incomplete abortion can occur without obvious symptoms.

Warning Signs — When to Seek Emergency Care

Most recoveries are straightforward. But certain symptoms mean you need medical attention immediately.

Call our 24/7 emergency line immediately on +256 759 983 865 if you experience any of the following:
  • Heavy bleeding — soaking 2 full pads per hour, or using 3 full pads in a day
  • Severe abdominal pain that is not relieved by your painkillers
  • Fever above 38°C, or fever with chills
  • Foul-smelling discharge from the vagina
  • Fainting, extreme dizziness, or weakness
  • Vomiting that will not stop

Do not wait to see if symptoms improve on their own. Delaying care can be dangerous — but receiving care early almost always resolves the problem safely.

Emotional Recovery

Emotional responses to abortion vary widely. Some women feel relieved. Others feel sad, confused, or nothing at all. Every reaction is normal.

There is no "right way" to feel. What matters is that you have support if you need it.

At Frontlane Medical Center, we provide:

  • On-site counselling — speak with a trained counsellor confidentially
  • A written emotional recovery guide — practical steps for processing your experience at your own pace

If you are struggling emotionally, please reach out. You don't have to face this alone.

Self-Care During Recovery

Simple steps help your body heal faster:

  • Rest when tired — you don't need bed rest, but listen to your body
  • Stay hydrated — drink plenty of water
  • Eat well — iron-rich foods help if you've lost blood
  • Use sanitary pads — avoid tampons for the first 2 weeks to reduce infection risk
  • Avoid sexual activity until bleeding has fully stopped and your follow-up visit is complete
  • Avoid swimming and baths — showers are fine
  • Take your medication as prescribed
  • Attend your follow-up appointment

Family Planning After Abortion

Fertility can return quickly after an abortion — often before your next period. If you don't want to become pregnant again, contraception is important.

We provide family planning counselling at your follow-up visit. Options include:

  • Oral contraceptive pills
  • Injectable contraception
  • Implants
  • Intrauterine devices (IUDs)
  • Condoms
  • Emergency contraception (for future use)

Our team will help you choose an option that fits your health, lifestyle, and preferences.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does bleeding last after an abortion?

After a medical abortion, bleeding typically lasts 3–5 days, similar to a period. After a surgical abortion, bleeding is usually light spotting only. Light spotting may continue for a few days afterward.

When can I return to work?

Most women return to work within 1–2 days after a medical abortion and about 2 days after a surgical abortion. If your work is physically demanding, give yourself an extra day or two.

When can I have sex again?

Wait until bleeding has fully stopped and your follow-up visit at 14 days is complete. This reduces the risk of infection.

When can I exercise again?

Light activity can resume within a few days. Wait until bleeding has stopped before returning to strenuous exercise, heavy lifting, or intense workouts.

Can I get pregnant again before my next period?

Yes. Fertility can return within weeks, often before your first period after abortion. Use contraception if you don't want to become pregnant again.

What if I think something is wrong?

Call our 24/7 emergency line on +256 759 983 865 immediately. Do not wait to see if symptoms improve — early care almost always resolves problems safely.
